## 👁️ Core Theoretical Pillars

Our research operates within three interconnected conceptual frameworks:
1. **Tech-Realism:** A de-politicized approach to geopolitics, asserting that sovereignty in the 21st century is defined by the control over technological layers rather than physical borders.
2. **Cybernetic Baroque:** Analytical framework exploring the post-techno-utopian era, characterized by hyper-fragmentation, digital enclosures, and algorithmic feudalism.
3. **VSCI Index (Vivien Yor Sovereignty & Connectivity Index):** A quantitative tool designed to measure a state's real digital and infrastructure sovereignty.

---

## 📊 VSCI Mathematical Framework

The VSCI Index measures system vulnerability across three operational tiers (Hardware component base, Cloud & software environment, Data logistics). 

For example, the mathematical model for **Border Perimeter Security ($VSCI_{border}$)** is defined as:

$$VSCI_{border} = w_1 \cdot M_{supply} + w_2 \cdot M_{digital} + w_3 \cdot M_{logistics}$$

Where:
* $M_{supply}$: Hardware supply-chain vulnerability coefficient (RISC-V vs proprietary foreign components).
* $M_{digital}$: Isolation and independence of the software/AI model layer (Local SLMs vs foreign APIs).
* $M_{logistics}$: Reliability and cryptographic resilience of data transmission and routing networks.

*Note: If $VSCI_{border} > 0.75$, the entity shifts into the "Vulnerable Sovereign" status, where critical infrastructure becomes prone to remote containment or infiltration.*
