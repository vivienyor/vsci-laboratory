### Core Theoretical Framework

The research focuses on structural shifts in the global system, specifically examining the deep nexus between **technology**, **resources**, and **geopolitical alliances**.

#### Key Original Concepts:
*   **[[Techno-Realism]]** — Analytical approach evaluating tech power through the lens of classical realism.
*   **[[Cyber-Baroque]]** — Conceptual framework exploring the complex, fragmented, and ornamental nature of modern digital power structures.
*   **[[Technological Westphalia]]** — The ongoing fragmentation of the global digital space into sovereign state-controlled technological zones.
*   **VSCI Methodology & Index** — A proprietary index and framework designed to measure and analyze technological sovereignty.
